The "Times" says that the "Punch" of the present day is not the "Punch" of forty years ago. It is not the same in spirit, nor in style, nor in matter. It has lost its originality, its freshness, its piquancy, its pungency, its point. It has become tame, and commonplace, and dull. MRS. BARBAULD'S poems all breathe the same spirit of liberty and humanity; without interfering with political questions beyond the limits that her sex marked out for her, she was well known to sympathize with the supporters of liberal opinions, and her feelings are beautifully expressed in many of her verses.
